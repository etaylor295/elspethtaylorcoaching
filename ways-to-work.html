Ways to work

There's no single
right way to do this.

Sometimes you need space to think. Sometimes challenge. Sometimes accountability. Sometimes you need to get outside, move and see what emerges. We'll find a way of working that fits you.

1:1 Coaching

A deeper commitment to yourself

Usually beginning with a discovery conversation, then a six-session commitment — enough time to explore what's happening, experiment with change and build real momentum. Structure, intention and accountability, but always bespoke. Not a rigid six-step programme.

Walk + Talk

Move your body, move your thinking

Coaching outdoors, side by side. Sometimes moving your body helps move your thinking. This can be part of ongoing coaching or a standalone conversation.

One-off Sessions

Bring the thing

For a decision, a challenge, a particular moment or something you're stuck on. Bring the thing. We'll work on it.

Groups + Workshops

Thinking, together

Interactive experiences for groups, sports clubs, teams, communities and organisations — virtual, in person or outdoors.

Programmes

Longer, shared journeys

Deeper work around particular challenges and transitions. Signature territories: Women in Flux and Stay in the Game.

Speaking + Conversations

For rooms that want to think

Keynotes, panels, events, facilitated conversations, leadership sessions and corporate or sports events. Territories: Something's Changing · Stay in the Game · Women in Flux · You Don't Need a New You.
