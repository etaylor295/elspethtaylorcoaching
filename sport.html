Sport

Stay in the game.

Perform. Return. Reconnect. Or simply keep doing what you love.

  • Maybe you want to perform better.
  • Maybe you've lost confidence.
  • Maybe competition suddenly feels different.
  • Maybe you're returning after injury or time away.
  • Maybe your body has changed.
  • Maybe you've stopped enjoying something you used to love.
  • Maybe you simply want sport and movement to stay part of your life for a very long time.

Performance matters. But it isn't the whole story.

Sport can be confidence, identity, health, friendship, challenge, belonging, joy, competition and connection.

The goal isn't always win more. Sometimes it's keep playing.

I've experienced competition from the inside — the confidence, fear, pressure, enjoyment, frustration, winning and losing that come with it. My coaching isn't about telling you how competitive you should be. It's about helping you find the relationship with sport that works for you now.

Racketball coach · multiple national racketball champion · international-level padel player · experienced competitor.
