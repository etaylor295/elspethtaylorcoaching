Women in Flux

A different conversation
about midlife and
menopause at work.

We talk a lot about the symptoms of menopause. We talk much less about the person experiencing them. Women in Flux explores the broader human transition that can accompany midlife and menopause: identity, confidence, ambition, energy, relationship with work, changing priorities, body, performance, possibility and what comes next.

Positioned as coaching, facilitated exploration and organisational support that complements — rather than replaces — clinical menopause education, medical care or specialist support.
